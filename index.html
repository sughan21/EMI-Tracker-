📱 How to Get Automatic Reminders on Your Phone

Fast 2-minute setup for iPhone and Android

1

Install as a Mobile App (PWA)

Open this page on your phone browser:

  • Android (Chrome): Tap the 3 dots menu (⋮) → tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install App".
  • iPhone (Safari): Tap the Share button (⎋) → scroll down → tap "Add to Home Screen".
2

Enable Push Notification Alarms

Tap "Enable Alerts" at the top. Allow notifications when prompted by your browser so LoanPulse can ring alerts before your due date.

3

Sync to Phone Calendar (Universal Alarms)

Tap "Download .ICS Calendar File" or "Google Calendar" on each loan. This creates an automatic repeating monthly event with sound alarms 1 day and 2 hours before the due time.

4

WhatsApp 1-Click Payment Alerts

Click the WhatsApp icon on any loan card anytime to instantly send a cleanly formatted reminder with amount and payment details to your own chat or family member.

5
